Research track

Real research with an active researcher, on a real question. It's small, it opens rarely, and we will never promise you a paper.

What it is

You join a project an active researcher is actually working on: reading, building skills, designing experiments, running them, and writing. If the work turns out to be good enough to publish, great. If not, you still learned how research is really done, from the inside.

We only open a spot when a mentor genuinely has room for one more person. That's why it's small and why we won't list ten disciplines we can't staff.

What it isn't

  • A guaranteed publication, or a name added to someone else's paper.
  • A short “project” packaged to look like research.
  • Something you can buy your way into.

How it works

  1. Leave your research profile

    Background, interests, anything you've done.

  2. We contact you only if there's a real match

    Usually there isn't. We won't send a sales follow-up.

  3. A short trial task

    So both sides can see whether it works.

  4. Start, with a milestone agreed up front

    No tuition. A ¥1,000 (US$150) deposit, returned when you reach the agreed milestone, for example finishing the experiments and a first draft.

Leave your research profile
