FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is it really free?
There's no tuition. Admitted students pay a ¥1,000 (US$150) deposit, which is returned in full within 14 days after Demo Day if you took part honestly. Details are on the transparency page.
Why a deposit at all?
Free programs lose most of their participants halfway, and every empty seat is one another applicant could have used. The deposit keeps people committed. It isn't income: kept deposits only pay running costs, and we publish the ledger.
What if my product fails?
You still get your deposit back. The deposit depends on taking part honestly, not on results. A well-documented failure counts as a Completed outcome and is worth more than a made-up success.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You'll build with AI coding tools, and week 0 sets everything up. You do need to be willing to learn by doing, and to put in about 10 hours a week.
Who can apply?
Cohort one is for undergraduate and master's students at universities in mainland China. Students elsewhere: email us and we'll tell you when other regions open.
Who runs STARC, and who reviews my work?
A small, unpaid volunteer team. We don't list names yet; we'd rather be judged by our published rules and our students' public work. AI helps summarize and draft feedback, that's always labeled, and a person reads everything before it's sent.
Will I get a certificate or a recommendation letter?
No certificate. Your proof is a public link to your product, your numbers and your logs. We don't sell recommendation letters.
Who owns what I build?
You do, 100%. STARC takes no equity, no revenue share and no credit.
Will my build logs be public?
Only if you agree, one log at a time, with your name or anonymously. You can withdraw a published log.
How is this different from paid programs?
We don't charge tuition, we don't promise outcomes, we don't do the work for you, we don't sell follow-up courses, and we publish our rules and our money. If a program won't tell you those things, ask why.
What happened to the US$300 fee?
STARC used to charge a US$300 operating fee for research matching. We've dropped it. Both tracks are now tuition-free with a refundable deposit.
Does STARC help publish papers for students?
No. The research track puts you inside real research. If the work merits publication, that's the outcome of the work, never something we sell or promise.

Something not covered? Email info@starc.institute.
