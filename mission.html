Why STARC

Shipping, not simulating.

Why a small nonprofit, why no tuition, and why we moved from papers to products.

The problem we saw

Across the “background-boosting” industry, students and their families pay large fees for a research internship, a competition package or a name on a paper. Some of these programs are real. Many sell the appearance of experience: a certificate, a polished project someone else mostly did, a co-authorship that doesn't stand up to a single question in an interview.

The students hurt most are the ones who can't afford to play, and the ones who did pay and got nothing real.

Why we moved from papers to products

STARC started as a research-mentorship platform. We still believe in real research, and we keep a small research track. But papers are losing their value as a signal, and mentor time doesn't scale.

What's still hard to fake is a product that works and that real people use, along with an honest record of how it was built. AI tools now let one student build what used to take a team. So our main program is now the one-person company cohort.

What we hold ourselves to

No tuition

A refundable deposit keeps people committed. Nobody is paid from student money.

Talent over background

Applications are reviewed with school names removed.

Open by default

Rules, money and outcomes are published, including the bad numbers.

Honest about our size

We're small. We won't claim mentors, partners or results we don't have.
