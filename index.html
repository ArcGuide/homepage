Why we exist

Students are paying for experience that isn't real.

Paid “research internships” and résumé packages often cost tens of thousands of yuan. Too often what comes back is a certificate, a name on a paper nobody reads, or a project someone else did.

Meanwhile, papers are losing their value as a signal. What is still hard to fake is a thing that works and that real people use, together with an honest record of how you built it. That is what STARC helps you make.

How it works

From application to Demo Day

  1. Apply with something you made

    Plus a 48-hour task: build and publish a one-page tool with any AI tool. We review applications with school names removed.

  2. Get admitted, then pay a refundable deposit

    ¥1,000 (US$150). Never before admission.

  3. Build for 8 weeks

    Ship, find users, write one build log a week. One hour of group review each week.

  4. Demo Day, then your deposit comes back

    In full within 14 days, even if your product didn't take off.

What we don't do

The list commercial programs won't publish

  • No ghost-building or ghost-writing. You do the work. We review it.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. Not funding, not revenue, not admissions, not publications.
  • No upsells. No follow-up courses, paid communities or "advanced tracks".
  • No paid recommendation letters or name-on-paper deals.
  • No payment before admission. Anyone asking for money in STARC's name before you are admitted is not us.
