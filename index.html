Why we exist

Students are paying for experience that isn't real.

Pay-to-play research programs, consultant-built “passion projects” and pay-to-publish journals sell students the look of experience. Too often what comes back is a certificate, a name on a paper nobody reads, or a project someone else did.

Admissions readers and recruiters have learned to discount that kind of line. What is still hard to fake is a thing that works and that real people use, together with an honest record of how you built it. That is what STARC helps you make.

Programs

Two tracks, one rule: real output.

Main program

One-Person Company (OPC) cohort

12 students, 8 weeks. Use AI to build a small real product, launch it, find real users and keep a weekly build log. Graduation is judged by evidence, not a certificate.

How it works

From application to Demo Day

  1. Apply with something you made

    Plus a 48-hour task: build and publish a one-page tool with any AI tool. We review applications with school names removed.

  2. Get reviewed on what you made

    Every application is read by a person. Everyone hears back either way.

  3. Build for 8 weeks

    Ship, find users, write one build log a week. One hour of group review each week.

  4. Demo Day

    Show the live product, the real numbers and the full log, including what didn't work.

What we don't do

The list commercial programs won't publish

  • No ghost-building or ghost-writing. You do the work. We review it.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. Not funding, not revenue, not admissions, not publications.
  • No recommendation letters or authorship for sale.
  • No made-up results. We won't claim mentors, partners or outcomes we don't have.
