How it works

How STARC works

We don't list names yet, so we'd rather you judge us by our rules. Here is what happens after you apply, what we won't do, and how we use AI. If anything here is unclear, tell us.

Process

What happens after you apply

  1. Applications close

    Applications stay open for about three weeks.

  2. Review

    Every application is read by a person, with school names removed. AI only organizes the materials; it doesn't score anyone.

  3. Decision

    12 admitted, 3 on a waitlist. Everyone hears back either way.

  4. Week 0 begins

    A set-up week: tools installed, one line of code live on the internet, three candidate problems.

  5. Eight weeks, then Demo Day

    One build log a week, one hour of group review a week, and a public showcase at the end.

What we don't do

In writing

  • No ghost-building or ghost-writing. You do the work. We review it.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. Not funding, not revenue, not admissions, not publications.
  • No recommendation letters or authorship for sale.
  • No made-up results. We won't claim mentors, partners or outcomes we don't have.
How we use AI

For summaries and drafts, never for judgment

AI summarizes the weekly build logs so reviewers can spend their time on judgment. It may draft written feedback; every AI-drafted piece says so, and a person reads it before it's sent.

AI never decides who is admitted.

Who runs STARC

A small team

STARC is run by a small team, and we don't list names yet. We'd rather be judged by the rules on this page and by our students' public work than by titles you can't verify.

Questions or concerns: info@starc.institute.
