Where kept deposits go

Running costs only, published every cohort

Kept deposits pay for the domain, hosting and tool subscriptions, and nothing else. They never go to any person. Whatever isn't spent rolls over to the next cohort.

After every cohort we publish a ledger here: deposits collected, returned and kept, and every expense.

Process

What happens after you apply

  1. Application closes

    Applications stay open for about three weeks.

  2. Review

    Every application is read by a person, with school names removed. AI only organizes the materials; it doesn't score anyone.

  3. Decision

    12 admitted, 3 on a waitlist. Everyone hears back either way.

  4. Deposit

    Payment details are sent only to admitted students.

  5. Week 0 begins

    Set-up week. Leaving now, or during week 1, means a full refund.

What we don't do

In writing

  • No ghost-building or ghost-writing. You do the work. We review it.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. Not funding, not revenue, not admissions, not publications.
  • No upsells. No follow-up courses, paid communities or "advanced tracks".
  • No paid recommendation letters or name-on-paper deals.
  • No payment before admission. Anyone asking for money in STARC's name before you are admitted is not us.
Who runs STARC

A small, unpaid team

STARC is run by a small volunteer team. Nobody is paid, and we don't list names yet. We'd rather be judged by the rules on this page and by our students' public work than by titles you can't verify.

Reviews may use AI to summarize and draft. Every AI-drafted piece of feedback says so, and a person reads it before it's sent.

Questions or concerns: info@starc.institute.
